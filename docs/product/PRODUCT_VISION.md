# Yway — Product Vision

## 1. Product purpose

Yway is a youth-first career discovery and growth ecosystem for Myanmar.

It helps a young person experience realistic parts of work before making a career decision, then reflect, choose a reversible direction, learn, practice, build appropriately labeled evidence, and access trusted opportunities when available.

Yway is not a job board, recruitment agency, placement company, or employer-first marketplace.

The core journey is:

**Explore careers → Try realistic work → Reflect → Choose direction → Learn → Practice → Build evidence → Access opportunities when available**

Youth value comes first.

The journey must remain meaningful without employers, jobs, Employer Quests, or recruitment opportunities.

The primary user outcome is completing a meaningful career experiment and taking a real next action.

App opens, time spent, registrations, streaks, and content views are not the main outcome.

---

## 2. Audience, language, and access

- Yway is for young people in Myanmar, initially focused roughly on ages 18–25.
- Public access is 18+.
- Any 16–17 pathway requires separately reviewed safeguarding and consent.
- During product shaping, information architecture, UX, design, content drafting, and implementation, Simple English is the canonical working language.
- Before public release, all user-facing launch content is localized into natural Burmese, with familiar English retained where it improves comprehension.
- Fluent Burmese review and target-user comprehension validation are mandatory release gates.
- First career value must be available without mandatory login.
- The youth experience is Android-first.

---

## 3. Product principles

### 3.1 Try before choosing

Do not ask uncertain young people to choose a career from descriptions alone.

Let them safely experience realistic parts of the work first.

---

### 3.2 Guidance, not destiny

A trial produces clues, not a permanent career verdict.

Direction stays provisional and reversible.

Yway must not produce:

- a universal best career
- a career-fit percentage
- an employability score
- a hidden candidate score
- deterministic career ranking

---

### 3.3 Keep different signals separate

Self-reported interest, observed behavior, work preferences, and practical constraints are separate lenses.

One must not silently replace another.

Exploration, practice, and verified assessment are also distinct.

One evidence level must never be presented as another.

---

### 3.4 Youth value before employer value

Employers and opportunities come after useful youth value exists.

Employer participation must not distort the core career-exploration journey.

---

### 3.5 Guided, not rigid

After a meaningful trial, a user may:

- go deeper
- try another career
- compare when useful
- pause

Comparison is not a mandatory gate.

---

### 3.6 Increase reality before commitment

Each next step should increase real-world exposure before it increases commitment.

A career experiment uses:

**Question → Action → Timebox → What to notice → Reflection → Next fork**

---

## 4. Core exploration experience

The youth experience begins with immediate career discovery and a short realistic work trial without mandatory login.

Career previews should clearly communicate:

- what the user will try
- expected time
- language needs
- connectivity needs
- practitioner-review status
- sponsorship, when relevant

The standardized **Career Experience Pack** is the core exploration content unit.

A pack may contain:

- career reality
- working conditions
- pressures
- tradeoffs
- realistic task simulations
- conversations
- prioritization
- document review
- error spotting
- workflow decisions
- short work samples
- reflection prompts
- contextual qualitative feedback
- Burmese-first essentials
- practical English vocabulary
- related practice variants
- transparent rubrics
- an optional structured practitioner experience
- a reversible next experiment
- explicit evidence level
- provenance
- version
- limitations

Exploration feedback should be qualitative and contextual rather than a visible numeric career score.

---

## 5. Content provenance

AI may assist with:

- drafting
- critique
- scenario generation
- localization
- internal review

AI is not a verified practitioner and does not certify workplace reality.

Content provenance must distinguish:

- AI-assisted work
- founder-reviewed work
- practitioner-reviewed work

A qualified practitioner remains the final content-quality gate for a production-quality Career Experience Pack.

---

## 6. Learning, practice, and evidence

Learning follows:

**Try → Feedback → Short lesson → Practice variant → Improvement → Next action**

Owned exploration and practice should remain useful offline.

Later synchronization must be designed so local youth work is not destroyed.

---

## 7. Evidence Portfolio

The private, user-owned Evidence Portfolio keeps the following categories separate:

1. **Exploration signals**
   Low-stakes evidence from career exploration.

2. **Practice evidence**
   Repeated behavior and improvement during practice.

3. **Verified assessment evidence**
   Evidence from a separately approved assessment process.

4. **User-added work**
   Work samples, experience, education, and practical constraints.

5. **Employer Quest work**
   Contextual evidence from a real employer challenge, when that layer exists.

Completion records must state exactly what was completed.

They must not be presented as:

- professional certification
- hiring evidence
- verified capability

unless appropriate support exists.

Users control what they:

- organize
- hide
- export
- selectively share

Provenance must remain intact.

---

## 8. Ecosystem boundaries

The following parts may connect to the youth journey, but they are distinct and must not become a mandatory funnel.

### 8.1 Practitioner Experiences

Practitioners may provide structured learning and support such as:

- career-reality sessions
- guided practice
- workshops
- Q&A
- scoped work-sample review

Participation does not automatically certify capability.

Payment must not buy:

- favorable evaluation
- better direction
- ranking
- hiring priority

---

### 8.2 Employer Quests

An Employer Quest is a real-world business challenge supplied by a vetted employer and reviewed and structured by Yway.

It is not:

- a normal exploration simulation
- a course
- a job post
- a hiring competition

Completing a Quest does not automatically:

- create candidate status
- prove capability
- guarantee hiring

---

### 8.3 Opportunities and hiring

Recruitment begins only when a user explicitly and voluntarily applies for a real opportunity.

The following actions do not make a user a candidate:

- exploring a career
- browsing opportunities
- completing an Employer Quest

Private exploration, reflections, ordinary practice signals, and unshared evidence must not automatically reach employers.

Youth must never pay recruitment or placement fees.

---

## 9. Trust, privacy, and safety

- Youth profiles and Evidence Portfolios are private and have no public URL.
- Sharing is purpose-specific and user-controlled.
- Users should be able to inspect what is being shared, with whom, and for what purpose before submission.
- Employer Quest submission and employment application are separate consent purposes.
- Employers must not gain access to private exploration, reflections, ordinary practice signals, or the wider private portfolio without explicit user action.
- Sponsorship must be disclosed.
- Sponsorship must not buy editorial control, private youth data, ranking, or favorable treatment.
- There is no behavioral advertising.
- User data is not sold.
- There is no pay-to-rank placement.
- Communication should be contextual and scoped rather than unrestricted private messaging.
- Any minor pathway requires separately reviewed safeguarding, consent, and access boundaries.

---

## 10. Experience and accessibility

Yway should feel:

- optimistic
- grounded
- capable
- Myanmar-aware

It should feel like a trusted young mentor.

It should not feel like:

- childish gamification
- corporate HR software
- school grading
- generic Silicon Valley career imagery

### Language and localization

- Product work uses Simple English as the canonical working language.
- Public UI uses natural Burmese plus familiar English where appropriate.
- The product remains localization-ready throughout design and implementation.
- Burmese review and comprehension testing are mandatory before public launch.

### Myanmar context

Myanmar context should appear through:

- real language
- work settings
- names
- locations
- relevant visual details

It should not rely on decorative cultural clichés.

### Progress

Progress should use:

- journey steps
- completion moments
- meaningful work artifacts

It should not use:

- points
- competitive streaks
- leaderboards

### Visual content

Visual content should favor:

- lightweight distinctive illustration
- authentic practitioner portraits

It should avoid:

- stock-photo galleries
- video-first screens
- generic corporate career imagery

### Accessibility

Yway requires:

- light theme
- dark theme
- Android text scaling to 200%
- strong contrast
- large touch targets
- screen-reader support
- reduced motion
- correct Burmese wrapping
- correct reading order
- clear offline states
- clear synchronization states

Motion should be brief and explanatory.

Youth layouts are Android-first at compact and large-phone sizes.

---

## 11. Explicit exclusions

Yway does not include:

- an open creator marketplace
- an open coaching marketplace
- a public social feed
- followers
- popularity metrics
- unrestricted messaging
- an open-ended AI career chatbot
- dynamic personality scoring
- opaque ranking
- universal career-fit scores
- employability scores
- hidden candidate scores
- employer access to private exploration
- employer access to ordinary practice signals
- automatic applications
- automatic candidate rejection
- pay-to-rank placement
- Employer Quest competition leaderboards
- automatic candidate creation
- a generic course catalog
- undifferentiated job-board aggregation
- unsupported certificates
- unsupported assessment claims
- behavioral advertising
- sale of user data
- mandatory account creation before first value

---

## 12. Product integrity

Yway's product value must remain understandable and useful even when no employer, job opportunity, Employer Quest, or recruitment pathway is available.

The product must preserve clear distinctions between:

**Exploration ≠ Practice ≠ Verified Assessment**

and:

**Employer Quest ≠ Employment Application**

The platform should help young people increase their understanding of work before increasing commitment.

Yway guides exploration and growth.

It does not decide a young person's future for them.
