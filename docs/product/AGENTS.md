# Product Documentation Instructions

- Apply the authority and owner-approval rules in root `AGENTS.md`.
- For an authorized product-document edit, read only the exact affected Product Vision and Product
  Contract sections.
- Keep contract IDs, classifications, and source traceability stable unless the authorized task
  explicitly requires a change.
- Do not convert audit findings, implementation convenience, or architecture preferences into
  product meaning.
- Recheck the repository-wide product, privacy, evidence, provenance, age, and offline guardrails
  after any product-document change.
