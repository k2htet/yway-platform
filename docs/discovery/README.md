# Discovery

## Purpose

This directory stores product-learning history: user research observations, experiments,
hypotheses, evidence, and what Yway learned from them.

## Authority

Discovery is non-authoritative. It does not override [Product Vision](../product/PRODUCT_VISION.md)
or [Product Contracts](../product/PRODUCT_CONTRACTS.md), and it does not automatically create
requirements.

## Separation model

- **Evidence:** What was observed.
- **Interpretation:** What we think the observation may mean.
- **Hypothesis:** What we want to test.
- **Decision:** An approved product direction or implementation decision.

## Learning flow

Evidence<br>
↓<br>
Interpretation<br>
↓<br>
Hypothesis update<br>
↓<br>
Approved decision<br>
↓<br>
Possible PRD, Contract, or Vision change

## AI-agent guidance

Use discovery artifacts to understand product-learning history. Do not treat hypotheses as
requirements or user feedback as automatic product authority. Do not change Product Vision or
Product Contracts based only on discovery notes.
